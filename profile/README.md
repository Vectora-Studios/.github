# Vectora Studios

**Independent Roblox development studio.**

Vectora Studios is an independent development studio focused on building Roblox experiences, developing supporting technology, and creating tools that help power our projects.

We believe in thoughtful design, solid engineering, and creating experiences that feel purposeful. Our work brings together game development, systems engineering, and a growing collection of technology developed under the Vectora name.

---

### What We Do

- **Roblox Development** — Designing and developing interactive experiences for the Roblox platform.
- **Framework Development** — Building and maintaining our own development technology through Vectora Framework.
- **Developer Tooling** — Exploring tools and supporting systems that improve our development workflow.
- **Technical Research** — Experimenting with ideas, technologies, and approaches that can shape future projects.

### Our Technology

**Vectora Framework**

Our development ecosystem is powered by Vectora Framework, our own framework for Roblox development. It provides a foundation for the systems and technology behind our projects.

### Our Repositories

This organisation is home to our projects, tools, experiments, and other development-related work.

Each repository has its own purpose and documentation. Check individual repositories for information about their functionality, development status, and usage.
